# Riley Grotenhuis

Laravel developer in Kansas City, MO.

I build web apps with Laravel on the backend and Vue and TypeScript on the frontend, tested with Pest and shipped on Laravel Cloud.

Most of what I make starts as something I wanted for myself or my friends: a pick'ems league for the NFL season, companion apps for the games we play, and small tools for planning a day or keeping a budget. I like projects that are small enough to finish and useful enough that people keep opening them.
